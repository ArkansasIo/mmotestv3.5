# Changelog

Changes recorded here apply to **Eldoria: Realms at War**. Add entries when behavior or user-facing content changes; do not assign a release number or date until a release is actually made.

## Unreleased

### Added
- Added the Adventurer’s Handbook: ten original callings with three paths each, a local character sheet, d20 ability checks and saves, proficiency scaling, level advancement, and a harmless practice encounter.
- Arcane Codex administration with five founder schools, classes, subclasses, and twenty lore-first spells; first-run seeding preserves existing non-empty browser catalogs.
- Expanded spellcraft with ten elemental schools and ninety spells; each elemental school has three classes, nine subclasses, and nine ranked spells with targets, effects, types, subtypes, stats, and substats. Added the player Grimoire, Crown-priced scroll learning and sales, Leyline Aether casting, local practice effects, mastery, and a casting chronicle. Versioned seed migration preserves existing Codex entries.
- Added the Eldoria World Bible covering its peoples, crowns, regions, history, creatures, and five founder schools of magic.
- Added OGame 0.84 fleet distance, travel-time, and deuterium formulas to expedition dispatch previews; successful launches consume fuel and use calculated round-trip time.
- Added the Craft Guild: 14 fantasy professions, 70 level-gated recipes, gathering materials, a persistent satchel, and wearable equipment slots with a 1–100 skill cap.
- Added a Mining ore-vein survey and shared ore stockpile for nine fantasy ores, each with mine class, subclass, type, subtype, depth, region, lore, required skill, and crafting uses.
- Recast Warlords & Dungeon Raids as an Eldoria War Council with eight original Marches warlords, nine Apex dungeon bosses, a Dawnsong shrine, and Free March sabotage cells.

### Changed
- Rewrote the project documentation around Eldoria's fantasy setting and current browser-client architecture.
- Clarified that Firebase synchronization is limited and the PHP/MySQL backend is a separate scaffold.
- Recast the realm update, patch chronicle, and development credits in the Age of Embers voice; removed unsupported release/build claims and documented current prototype boundaries.

## History Note

Older entries described a previous *Universe Civilization* project identity and included feature claims that do not reliably describe the current Eldoria build. They are not carried forward as Eldoria release history. Future entries should be tied to verified repository changes.
